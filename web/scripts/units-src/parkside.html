Basic information is required from the three parties involved in the contract: (1) the resident, (2) the tenant (guardian), (3) the guarantor. The required documents include: ID or passport , contact details (email & phone) as well as tax information (where applicable) All requirements are sent to you in detail via email.
Three parties are involved in the lease: the resident (who lives in the unit), the renter (the person who on the contract signs as the payer of the rent), the guarantor. Only the renter and guarantor sign the contract. Signing can be completed digitally or physically. The lease is then officially registered and accepted through the Greek tax authority (AADE), with full guidance from our team.
Rent is paid monthly via bank transfer, following the detailed instructions you receive during signing.
The deposit is returned to you via bank transfer within fifteen (15) days from the end of your contract, provided all contract terms have been met.
